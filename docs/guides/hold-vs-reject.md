# VJ Hold vs Reject Guide

Use this when reviewing a Verified Jobs submission and deciding whether to pause it, reject it, or approve it.

## Quick rule

**Hold fixable posts. Reject posts that should not be allowed through.**

If the job looks real but needs clarification or cleanup, Hold it. If the job is scammy, unsuitable, duplicate, or not allowed under VJ rules, Reject it.

## Use Hold when the issue can be fixed

Hold a submission when it looks like a real job, but staff needs clarification or needs to clean up the submission before approving it.

Good reasons to hold:

- Job details or deliverables are too vague, but the user can explain them better
- Budget needs to be clarified, corrected, or converted into a proper per-project amount
- Currency is unclear
- Payment method is unclear or needs cleanup
- References/examples are missing or need to be added
- Client/agency disclosure is missing or unclear
- The post has useful info, but needs better wording before approval
- You need clarification from the submitter before deciding
- You want another staff member's opinion

Do **not** reject just because payment terms are missing. Payment terms are not required.

## Editable fields while held

Staff can edit these fields while a submission is on hold:

- Description / job details
- Budget
- Payment method
- Reference links
- Additional notes / clarification

After the submitter clarifies, update the held submission if needed, then approve or reject it.

## What happens after Hold

- No strike is added
- The submitter gets a DM asking them to clarify/open a ticket
- The review card turns gold
- Staff can edit the held submission
- The Hold button is replaced by Withdraw
- Approve and Reject stay available

If the submitter does not respond after about 48 hours, use **Withdraw**. Withdraw cancels the submission without adding a strike.

## Use Reject when the job should not be allowed

Reject when the issue is not just missing info or rough wording.

Good reasons to reject:

- Scam or suspicious behavior
- Not a paid job
- Wrong channel / not suitable for VJ
- Duplicate post while the original is still active
- Two separate jobs in one post
- Unrealistic requirements or expectations
- Unreliable payment model, like percentage of revenue/views
- Budget is below the minimum and the post should not be accepted
- Clear rule violation

Rejecting adds 1 strike and sends the submitter a DM with the selected reason.

## Crypto and payment notes

Crypto payment submissions are normally auto-declined by the bot before staff review, so staff usually should not need to reject them manually.

Payment terms are not required. Do not reject or hold only because payment terms are missing.

## Quick decision guide

1. Complete and acceptable? **Approve**.
2. Fixable with clarification or staff edit? **Hold**.
3. Not allowed, scammy, duplicate, or rule-breaking? **Reject**.
4. No response after hold? **Withdraw**.

When unsure, Hold first. It is better to pause, clarify, and edit than reject a fixable post.
